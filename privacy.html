Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 4, 2026

Dogdoku is a single-player puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use the game.

Information We Collect

Dogdoku does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or other direct contact information.

Local Game Data

The app stores gameplay progress, unlocked levels, star ratings, audio settings, theme settings, and language settings locally on your device. This information is used to operate the game and restore your progress.

Advertising

Dogdoku may show advertisements, including interstitial ads and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are optional and may be offered when you choose to retry a level after failing.

The app may use third-party advertising SDKs to load and display ads. These partners may process device identifiers, ad interaction data, approximate device information, and similar technical data to deliver, measure, and improve advertising, subject to their own privacy policies and your device settings.

App Tracking Transparency

Where required by Apple, the app may ask for permission before accessing tracking-related identifiers. You can change this choice at any time in your iOS settings.

Analytics

Dogdoku does not require a user account and does not include social features. If diagnostic or advertising-related information is processed by third-party SDKs, it is used for app operation, ad delivery, fraud prevention, measurement, and performance purposes.

Children's Privacy

Dogdoku is designed to be family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect direct personal contact information from children. Advertising partners may process limited technical data as described above.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your name, email address, or account information because the app does not collect those items. Advertising SDK providers may receive technical data necessary to provide ads and related services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local game progress can be removed by deleting the app from your device. Data processed by third-party advertising providers is handled according to their own retention and deletion practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes or if legal requirements change. Updates will be reflected by changing the effective date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the app developer through the support information provided on the App Store listing.
