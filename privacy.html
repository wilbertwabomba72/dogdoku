Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dogdoku handles information when you use the game.

Information We Collect

Dogdoku does not require account registration and does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or address.

The game may store local gameplay information on your device, such as unlocked levels, star ratings, sound settings, music settings, and vibration settings.

Advertising

Dogdoku may use third-party advertising SDKs to show interstitial or rewarded ads. These advertising partners may collect device identifiers, ad interaction data, and technical information to provide, measure, and improve advertising.

Rewarded ads are optional and may be used to retry a level after failure. If an ad is not available, the game continues without requiring an ad view.

Analytics and Diagnostics

The app may receive basic technical information through integrated SDKs, such as device type, operating system version, app version, crash data, and ad delivery status. This information helps maintain app stability and improve the game experience.

Children's Privacy

Dogdoku is designed as a family-friendly puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party SDK providers only for purposes such as advertising delivery, fraud prevention, measurement, diagnostics, and app functionality.

Data Retention

Local gameplay progress remains on your device unless you delete the app or clear app data. Third-party SDK providers may retain data according to their own privacy policies.

Your Choices

  • You can disable music, sound, and vibration in the game settings.
  • You can avoid optional rewarded ads by not selecting rewarded retry options.
  • You can delete the app to remove locally stored game progress from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
