Dogdoku

Cute puppy logic puzzles with warm, relaxing levels.

Place adorable pups on colorful boards, follow simple logic rules, and complete each level with careful thinking. Dogdoku is easy to learn, cozy to play, and perfect for a quick puzzle break.

How to Play

Place the pups

Tap the board to place a dog in the right spot and solve each level step by step.

Follow the rules

Use logic: one dog per color area, no duplicate row or column, and no touching pups.

Earn stars

Complete levels, unlock new boards, and improve your star ratings as puzzles get harder.
