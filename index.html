Dogdoku

Cute puppy logic puzzles

Dogdoku is a warm and cozy puzzle game where adorable puppies meet satisfying logic. Place pups carefully, follow simple rules, complete levels, and earn stars.

Puppy Theme Logic Puzzle Cozy Casual

How It Plays

Put one puppy in each color area, avoid placing puppies in the same row or column, and make sure puppies do not touch. The rules are easy to learn, while each new level brings a fresh challenge.

Features

  • Cozy puppy-themed logic puzzle gameplay
  • Warm illustrated mobile UI
  • 30 levels with locked, playable, and completed states
  • Star ratings and saved progress
  • Music, sound, and vibration settings
